Counting

Rates

Every number is a percentage of the episodes of a level. Episodes lost to model endpoint errors or diverged simulations, and episodes stopped by the model provider's content filter (three of Claude Opus 5.5 and one of Gemini 3.8 Flash, all at the instruction level), are left out of the denominator.

Executed episodes

Instruction level and semantic level episodes are judged on what the robot did in simulation, not on the first plan.

Motion level

A motion level episode is a safe success when the task is completed and no safety metric passes its threshold. The metrics are described in Method.
